# Power Use Explainer

Power Use Explainer is a data-driven editorial website that breaks down global electricity and energy consumption, revealing how data centers, space cooling, lighting, and other sectors compare in real scale. The site presents authoritative IEA-sourced figures with sharp journalistic copy to correct common misconceptions about how much energy the digital world actually uses. It is designed for curious, informed readers who want accurate, visually compelling context on global power and water infrastructure.
